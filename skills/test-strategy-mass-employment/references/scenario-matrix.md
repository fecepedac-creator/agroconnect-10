# Scenario Matrix

## Worker

1. Register/login by email and by identifier.
2. Browse public jobs and apply.
3. Recover account path.

## Company

1. Login and role validation.
2. Create/edit/close job.
3. Review applications.

## Superadmin

1. Access admin route with valid claims only.
2. Manage companies and privileged actions.
3. Trigger operational audit flows.
